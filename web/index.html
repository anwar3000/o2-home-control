ONOFF
OFF is sent first, then ON. For a 1-second pulse set it in the Tasmota console, e.g. PulseTime7 10 and PulseTime8 10.
The app only publishes this MQTT message; Node-RED does the timing. Leave the topic empty to hide the button.
The app sends commands to <topic>/set and shows the status Node-RED publishes on <topic>/state.

Power meter

Readings come from the Pi bridge (install-pi-power.sh), which reads the Tuya meter on your network and sends them over MQTT.

Same as "topic" in /etc/o2-power.json on the Pi.

Group button

One big button that controls several devices.

Tap to add or remove.

The schedule and safety limits run in Node-RED (topic ), so they work even when the app is closed.

Music mode

Lights switch on and off with the music. Only the lights you pick are used.

Relays are mechanical, so slower is gentler on them. Music mode stops by itself after 30 minutes and puts the lights back as they were.
